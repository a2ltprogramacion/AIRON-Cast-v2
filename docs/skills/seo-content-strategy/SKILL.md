# SEO Content Strategy (A2LT Standard)

This macro-skill drives the entire content lifecycle: from strategic planning and topical clustering to writing, keyword optimization, and continuous refreshing.

---

## 1. Content Planning & Topic Clustering

Before writing, establish topical authority through structured planning.

- **Topic Cluster Mapping:** Define one massive "Pillar Page" supported by highly specific "Cluster Pages" (subtopics, FAQs, Versus content).
- **Search Intent Alignment:** Map every planned URL to one of four intents: Informational, Navigational, Commercial, or Transactional.
- **Content Calendar:** Deliver a 30-60 day prioritization blueprint grouping formats, target word counts, and internal linking targets.

### The Content Outline Structure

Always generate outlines following this exact format:

```markdown
Title: [Main Topic]
Intent: [Commercial/Informational]
Word Count: [Target]

I. Introduction (Hook & Value Prop)
II. Main Section 1 (H2 + Primary KW)
A. Subtopic (H3)
III. Main Section 2...
```

---

## 2. SEO Content Writing Framework

When tasked to write or evaluate copy, adhere strictly to these principles:

- **Introduction (50-100 words):** Hook the reader instantly. Place the primary keyword naturally in the first paragraph.
- **Body Content:** Favor short paragraphs (2-3 sentences), bullet points for scannability, and semantic headings (H2/H3). E-E-A-T signals must be interwoven (first-hand experience, data citations).
- **Quality Standard:** Grade 8-10 reading level. Write for humans; structure for Google.

---

## 3. Keyword Strategist & Semantic Optimization (LSI)

Analyze draft text to prevent over-optimization while maximizing semantic relevance.

- **Density Guidelines:** Primary keyword target is `0.5% - 1.5%`. Keyword stuffing restricts rankings.
- **Entity Analysis:** Extract the primary "entities" (nouns/concepts associated with the topic) and inject LSI (Latent Semantic Indexing) keywords.
- **Delivery Format:**

```markdown
Primary: [keyword] (0.8% density, 12 uses)
LSI Keywords: [20-30 semantic variations to sprinkle]
Entities: [Related concepts to build authority]
```

---

## 4. Content Refresher (Preventing Decay)

SEO content is not static. Analyze existing, aging content for immediate refresh opportunities.

- **Refresh Priority Matrix:**
  - **High:** Pages losing rankings, stats older than 2 years, dates containing "2023" in the title.
  - **Medium:** Stagnant rankings for 6+ months, minor shifts in search intent, broken outbound links.
- **Actionable Refresh Tactics:**
  - Update all numerical data and statistics.
  - Inject new expert quotes to revitalize E-E-A-T.
  - Add newly relevant FAQs capturing rising "People Also Ask" metrics.
  - Modify the schema markup "dateModified" and the frontend publish date.

---

## 5. Execution Rule

Never just generate text. Provide the strategic overview (Intent, Keyword Density, Target Audience) alongside the actual content payload. Emphasize value-driven depth over generic fluff.
